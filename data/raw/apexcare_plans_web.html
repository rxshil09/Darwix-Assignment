ApexCare Health Coverage Tiers (2026 Edition)

ApexCare offers three primary health insurance tiers tailored to individual and family health protection needs across all 50 states.

ApexCare Bronze Tier (Essential Care)

Monthly Premium: Starting at $180/month.

Annual Deductible: $4,500 individual / $9,000 family.

In-Network Co-payment: $40 for primary care visits, $80 for specialist consultations.

Prescription Drug Coverage: Generic medications covered at 80% after deductible. Brand-name drugs require prior authorization.

Hospitalization: 70% coverage after deductible at contracted network hospitals.

Preventive Care: 100% covered with $0 co-payment (annual physicals, vaccinations, mammograms, well-child visits).

ApexCare Silver Tier (Balanced Care)

Monthly Premium: Starting at $320/month.

Annual Deductible: $2,500 individual / $5,000 family.

In-Network Co-payment: $25 for primary care visits, $50 for specialist consultations.

Prescription Drug Coverage: Tier 1 generics $10 co-payment; Tier 2 brand drugs $35 co-payment without deductible.

Hospitalization: 85% coverage after deductible at contracted network hospitals.

Cashless Inpatient Admission: Supported across 4,200+ partner network hospitals nationwide.

Emergency Room: $250 co-payment per visit (waived if admitted).

ApexCare Gold Tier (Premium Comprehensive)

Monthly Premium: Starting at $480/month.

Annual Deductible: $750 individual / $1,500 family.

In-Network Co-payment: $10 for primary care visits, $20 for specialist consultations.

Prescription Drug Coverage: Comprehensive 95% coverage on formulary drugs; $5 generic co-payment.

Hospitalization & Surgery: 100% covered after deductible up to $2,000,000 lifetime policy limit.

Zero Co-payment Network: Full cashless hospitalization at top-tier medical centers with private room allowance.

Included Wellness Perks: Unlimited telemedicine consultations 24/7, annual cardiac health screening, and mental health therapy (up to 20 sessions per year).

How to File a Cashless Claim at a Network Hospital

To access cashless hospitalization without out-of-pocket payment:

  1. Step 1 - Pre-Authorization: Present your ApexCare digital member ID card at the hospital's TPA / insurance helpdesk at least 48 hours prior to planned admission (or within 24 hours in emergency situations).
  2. Step 2 - Verification: The hospital submits the cashless pre-authorization form along with initial physician estimates to ApexCare claims desk.
  3. Step 3 - Instant Approval: ApexCare reviews and approves eligible expenses within 60 minutes for network hospitals.
  4. Step 4 - Discharge: Upon discharge, ApexCare settles the approved bill directly with the hospital. The member only pays non-medical expenses (such as registration fees or personal luxury items).
